📊 Power BI – Payment Health & Customer Retention Analytics
📌 Overview

This project analyzes payment performance, revenue health, and customer retention using Power BI.
The dashboard is designed to help businesses identify revenue risks, late payments, and churn drivers.

🧠 Business Problems Addressed

Are customers paying on time?

How much revenue is at risk due to late or failed payments?

Which customer segments drive MRR?

Where does churn happen in the customer lifecycle?

🗂 Dashboard Structure
🔹 Page 1: Payment Health Monitor

Total Collections

Average Days Overdue

On-Time Payment Rate

Failed / Late / Pending payment analysis

Monthly Paid vs Pending revenue trend

🔹 Page 2: Customer Retention Analytics

Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)

Active & At-Risk Customers

Churn Rate & trend by segment

Cohort retention matrix (M0–M12)

Revenue bridge & revenue distribution

High-risk customer identification
